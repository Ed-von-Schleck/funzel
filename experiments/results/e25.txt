  reading: the bound e8 uses is a cap times a correlation sum against
  a residual energy. On this dictionary one atom already explains most
  of the image, so the cap that admits it is of the order of ||y||,
  and the product overwhelms the residual before the search starts.
  Making the bound useful rather than merely non-zero would need the
  cap at 0.15-0.25 of the optimum's largest amplitude.

  3 of the 5 pre-registered claims failed, and they sharpen the
  result rather than overturning it. The bound is not uniformly
  vacuous: it drops below 1 at the last branching layer, and at N in
  [4] it certifies part of the way to the answer. But
  that is the layer where only one atom is left to choose, so nothing
  is pruned by it, and the certified fraction falls away at the larger
  budget. The instrument degrades as N grows, which is the direction
  an encoder moves in.
  What it responds to is the cap, not the search: on the same
  instances at the same node budget, widening the cap never narrows
  the gap and widens it wherever there is room left to widen.

  Theorem 11 is not what fails -- the polytope really does see N. What
  fails is that the only handle it gives is an amplitude cap, and on
  Gaussian blobs the amplitudes have no scale to cap at.
