  reading. Two explanations are excluded and one survives.
  Not the mean: projecting the constant out of every atom leaves the
  maximum coherence and the separable share where they were.
  Not scale nesting: the pairs above 0.9 are the same size to two
  decimals, which refutes the prediction this file was written with.
  What they are is near-coincident atoms of equal size at different
  ORIENTATIONS -- an elongated bump rotated by 30 degrees about almost
  the same centre still shares most of its mass with the original,
  because a positive bump has nothing to cancel against. That is why
  a localised negative surround helps and a constant offset does not,
  and it is a property of positive bumps that no choice of lattice
  removes: the orientations have to be there for the dictionary to fit
  edges at all.
